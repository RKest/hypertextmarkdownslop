Exact Term Naming Matrix: Workflows & Agents Across 10 Documentation Ecosystems

Evaluation of 18 exact, ungrouped terms (separating nouns and verbs such as run vs. runner, invoke vs. invocation, orchestrate vs. orchestration, execute vs. execution, agent vs. agentic_system, and session vs. interaction) across all 10 documentation sources linked in the OTel LLM SemConv WG #571 Naming Doc .

  • Whole-Bundle Requirement: A slice is Green only when the exact term refers to the bundle of work as a whole (all workflows as a whole on the left; a single-agent inference + 0..N tool call execution as a whole on the right). Referring to only one subtype of workflow (e.g. LoopAgent or Evaluator-Optimizer) or a single step/iteration inside a bundle is graded Red.
  • No Word Grouping: Distinct words and parts of speech are evaluated in their own dedicated columns.
3-State Grading Rules
1. Green: Term is present in the doc and refers to this concept as a whole (Left = Workflow, Right = Agent; Full Green = Both).
2. Grey: Term is not present in the doc.
3. Red: Term is present in the doc, but is used to refer to something else (e.g. only one of Workflow/Agent, a single LLM/tool call, a single step/iteration, one workflow subtype, or a multi-turn session).
1. Most Full-Green (Both WF & Agent)
run (5 🟢) · invoke (4 🟢) · runner (3 🟢)

run refers to both a workflow run and a single-agent run as a whole in 5 docs. invoke covers both in 4 docs (MAF, ADK, Gemini Enterprise, Bedrock), though in 5 other docs it means a single LLM/tool call (🔴). runner is Full Green in 3 docs and Grey (unclaimed) in 6 docs.

2. Noun vs. Verb Splits
invoke (4 🟢) vs. invocation (1 🟢)

Splitting words reveals big differences: while the verb invoke covers both workflows and agents in 4 docs, the noun invocation only covers both as a whole in 1 doc (Google ADK) and refers to single-agent or atomic LLM/tool calls in the other 9.

3. Why loop & step Have 0 Full-Greens
loop (0 Both, 8 Agent) · step (0 Green, 9 🔴)

In 8 docs, loop refers to a single-agent loop as a whole (Right 🟢), but for workflows it only ever refers to one subtype (LoopAgent, Evaluator-Optimizer), never workflows as a whole (Left 🔴). step is Full 🔴 in 9/10 docs because it always refers to an inner stage/iteration, never the bundle as a whole.

4. Unclaimed (Grey) Umbrella Terms
agentic_system (1 🟢, 8 ⚪) · runner (3 🟢, 6 ⚪)

Anthropic explicitly coins agentic_system for both workflows and agents as a whole, and it is completely absent (⚪ Grey, zero conflicts) in 8 of 10 docs. Conversely, session is Full 🔴 in 8/10 docs (always meaning a multi-turn history container).
